FlashChords Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

FlashChords (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

⸻

1. Information We Collect

FlashChords does not require users to create an account and does not collect personal information such as:
	•	Name
	•	Email address
	•	Phone number
	•	Payment information

However, the App may collect limited anonymous data to improve performance and usability.

⸻

2. Analytics Data

FlashChords uses Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App. This may include anonymous information such as:
	•	Which screens are viewed
	•	Feature usage (e.g., flashcard sessions completed)
	•	App version and device type

This data is collected in a way that does not directly identify you.

⸻

3. Crash and Performance Data

FlashChords may collect diagnostic information such as crash reports to improve stability and fix bugs. This may include:
	•	Crash logs
	•	Performance metrics

This information is used only for app improvement.

⸻

4. Tracking

FlashChords does not use data for advertising purposes and does not track users across other apps or websites.

⸻

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent user data. Anonymous analytics and diagnostic data may be processed by Firebase (Google) as a service provider.

⸻

6. Children’s Privacy

FlashChords does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

⸻

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
