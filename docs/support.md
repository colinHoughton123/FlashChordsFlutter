## Common questions

**Q: The upgrade screen says “Upgrade is not available right now.”**  
A: This usually means the in‑app purchase is not yet approved or available in your region. Please try again later.

**Q: I purchased Pro but it didn’t unlock.**  
A: Please open the app and try again. If it still doesn’t unlock, contact support with your device model and iOS version.

## App Info
FlashChords helps you practice and recall chords quickly through short, focused practice sessions.
